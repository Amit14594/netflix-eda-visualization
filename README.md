# Netflix — Data Exploration & Visualization

**Domain:** Media / Streaming  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
**Focus:** EDA + Business Insights for Content Strategy

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Analyze Netflix’s catalog (movies & TV shows) to generate insights that help decide:
- Which type of shows/movies to produce
- How to grow the business in different countries

**Dataset:** ~8,807 titles with attributes — type, title, director, cast, country, date added, release year, rating, duration, listed_in (genres), description.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Perform thorough Exploratory Data Analysis
2. Understand content mix (Movies vs TV Shows)
3. Analyze country-wise production and addition trends
4. Explore genres, ratings and duration patterns
5. Provide actionable business recommendations for content acquisition and regional growth

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Data cleaning (handling missing directors, cast, countries, etc.)

---

## 📊 Key Exploration Areas

- Movies vs TV Shows distribution over years
- Top producing countries
- Most popular genres and rating categories
- Content addition trends on the platform
- Duration analysis (movies in minutes, shows in seasons)

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations (Examples)

- Focus on high-demand genres in top markets
- Increase local language / regional content in high-growth countries
- Balance between movies and multi-season shows based on retention patterns

*(Detailed charts and insights are in the notebook / PDF report.)*
